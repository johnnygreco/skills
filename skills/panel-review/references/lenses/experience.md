# Experience

Can the intended users understand, use, and recover from the changed workflow?

- Walk the task from discovery to completion, including errors and recovery.
  Consider people, developers, and agents according to who uses the interface.
- Check whether inputs, outputs, defaults, feedback, and documentation make the
  contract clear without hidden context.
- Assess accessibility, interaction, and presentation for the relevant medium.
  Separate observed behavior from assumptions that need a rendered or live check.
- Check whether changes leave examples, instructions, or existing expectations
  misleading, and whether users can discover what they need to do differently.

Tie findings to a task users cannot complete, a consequential misunderstanding,
or avoidable loss of work. Keep taste and stylistic preferences out of the report.
