# Intent

Does the change deliver the requested outcome within the agreed scope?

- Compare the result with the user's request, acceptance criteria, and relevant
  specification. Identify missing, partial, or incorrectly interpreted behavior.
- Check whether additions serve the requested outcome or expand the assignment.
  Respect explicit non-goals and decisions already made.
- Distinguish an implementation gap from an ambiguous requirement. Use the user's
  request and known contracts when a formal specification is unavailable.
- Consider whether the intended outcome remains achievable through the complete
  workflow, including failure and recovery.

Cite the requirement and the part of the result that falls short. Keep intent
separate from standards compliance: following conventions does not establish that
the requested work was done.
